So what's this all about?

This is a resource page for BIPOC based out of the Portland, OR Metro area. This site is meant to serve as a local bulletin for resources, predominantly for and by BIPOC. Events, resources and more will be updated as frequently as time permits. If you are interested in adding a resource or have recommendations, please feel free to reach out at portlandmetrobipoc@protonmail.com or fill out the form page at the link below for submissions.
